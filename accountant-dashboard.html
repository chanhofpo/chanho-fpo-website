RTGS/NEFT can be saved Completed directly. Do not enter the same payment again in Payment Entry. Enter salary and commission payments in the separate form below.

To Mark Cleared an issued cheque, select the clearing date below. Cleared payments cannot be cancelled here; contact the Accountant or Officer to make corrections.
